Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to the current Serene Tiles iOS app

Serene Tiles is designed for offline play. The app does not require an account, show advertisements or send gameplay and profile information to developer-operated servers.

At a glance
  • No account or login.
  • No advertising or third-party advertising trackers.
  • No analytics SDK or developer-operated cloud synchronization in the current app.
  • Progress, preferences and optional profile details are saved locally.
  • Core gameplay does not require an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The app saves information needed to continue your experience, including game and level progress, current attempts, scores, mastery, available booster uses, selected collections and backgrounds, language, audio and motion preferences, and an optional player name and selected avatar. Local backup copies may be kept to help recover saved progress.

This is local storage, not a developer account or server database. Your optional name and avatar are used inside the app; there are no public profiles or social-sharing features in the current build.

Information we do not collect

The current app does not send this local gameplay, preference or profile information to us. It does not include advertising, analytics, account authentication or purchase SDKs. It does not ask you to provide an email address or phone number to play.

Internet access and Apple services

Gameplay and local saving work offline. If you choose an external action, such as opening an App Store review page or this policy website, your browser or Apple's service handles that interaction. Their privacy practices apply separately. Availability of the review action depends on the app's App Store listing.

Apple may separately process App Store transactions or reviews, device diagnostics and crash information according to your settings and Apple's policies. Device or computer backups may include app data depending on your backup settings. Serene Tiles does not operate its own cloud-sync service.

Retention, changes and deletion

Local data remains on your device until changed or removed. You can edit your profile and preferences in Settings. Deleting the app through iOS normally removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain it. Existing device or computer backups may retain earlier copies, and restoring a backup may restore app data. Manage those copies through the applicable Apple or computer backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app's local-only approach applies to all players. It does not provide account registration, messaging, advertising or a feature for submitting a child's personal information to us.

About this website

This policy describes the Serene Tiles app. Viewing this website is an online interaction with its hosting provider. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies to this website. Hosting and browser services may process technical request information to deliver the pages.

Policy updates

If the app's features or data handling change, this page will be updated with a new date. The current app is still being prepared for release.
